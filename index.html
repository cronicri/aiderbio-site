Core Technology

The website centers on what the materials actually show.

Instead of decorative language, the technology section is organized around the real hardware structure image and the actual AI analysis flow image.

Pillar 1. 3D Cell Culture Hardware

The hardware uses a hybrid single-well structure and port-pocket design to reduce structural disturbance during media exchange and keep cells and organoids positioned more consistently throughout an experiment.

  • Structural design — Separate zones for culture, port pocket, and media flow improve stability.
  • OrganoWell™ 96 — A scalable 96-well format built for practical lab use.
  • Prototype-driven development — The prototype itself anchors the productization roadmap.

Pillar 2. AI-Based 3D Cell Prediction System

The AI system ingests fluorescence images, extracts meaningful features automatically, and predicts cytotoxicity and cellular response as quantitative outputs that are easier for teams to interpret and compare.

  • Image input — Live/Dead fluorescence images classify cellular state.
  • AI feature extraction — Intensity, morphology, and pattern features are quantified.
  • Prediction and reporting — Toxicity and response are visualized through metrics and charts.
