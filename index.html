Why we exist

More than 95% of preclinical drugs still fail in human trials.

Most drug candidates that pass animal testing fail in clinical trials. The world is shifting toward Non-Animal Methods (NAMs), yet existing 3D cell cultures suffer from poor reproducibility, low cell recovery, and inconsistent results — limiting their use in real drug evaluation pipelines.

Core Technology

Two patented technologies.
One reliable pipeline.

Each technology was designed to solve a specific pain point in 3D drug evaluation — together they form a fully integrated discovery pipeline.

Pillar 1 · Hardware

Port Pocket™ — Hybrid single-well design that doesn't lose your cells.

Conventional 3D plates lose cells during media exchange and yield variable recovery. The hybrid hexagonal micro-pocket geometry — combining Gri3D-style array and 4Dcell-style anchor pillars — physically retains organoids in place, dramatically improving reproducibility.

Pillar 2 · Software

AI Toxicity Prediction — turn images into decisions.

A purpose-built end-to-end pipeline. Fluorescence images of Live/Dead-stained cells are processed by our AI segmentation engine, embedded into quantitative feature vectors, and translated into the three indicators teams actually need: migration, apoptosis, and toxicity.
